Structure

A clear framework for every event.

  1. 01

    Scheduled

    Every tournament has a published registration period and competition window.

  2. 02

    Simulated

    Competition orders do not execute through a live brokerage account and do not use customer funds.

    Defined

    Eligible instruments, data conditions, scoring, and rules are stated for each tournament.

Notice: The Caldamere Series uses simulated trading. Competition orders do not execute through a live brokerage account, and no customer funds are deposited or traded through the competition interface.
