Institutional Precision

A clear framework for every event.

  1. 01 / SCHEDULED

    Pre-defined Windows

    Every tournament has a published registration period and competition window. No continuous survivorship churn.

  2. 02 / SIMULATED

    Zero Capital Risk

    Competition orders execute exclusively through simulated market environments and never risk participant capital.

  3. 03 / DEFINED

    Standardized Rules

    Eligible instruments, data conditions, scoring metrics, and risk limits are locked and transparent for every participant.

Regulatory Notice: The Caldamere Series uses simulated trading. Competition orders do not execute through a live brokerage account, and no customer funds are deposited or traded through the competition interface. CFTC Rule 4.41 hypothetical performance disclosures apply.
