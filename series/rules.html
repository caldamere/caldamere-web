Rules Framework

Rules and participation criteria.

Published standards applied to every Caldamere tournament.

1. Simulated Trading Environment

All events utilize simulation. Orders do not interact with live markets, and no customer funds are handled.

2. Scoring & Metrics

Scoring formulas take into account cumulative returns alongside maximum drawdown thresholds.
