Competition Protocol

Rules & Risk Standards

Uniform parameters applied across all Caldamere tournament environments.

  1. RULE 01 // CAPITAL SIMULATION

    Zero Personal Risk

    Every participant receives an identical starting simulated cash allocation. No account funding or personal deposits are permitted.

  2. RULE 02 // DRAWDOWN RISK LIMITS

    Maximum Trailing Drawdown

    Each tournament specifies a hard trailing drawdown limit (typically 4% to 6%). Violating this threshold halts simulated execution privileges for the remainder of that event.

  3. RULE 03 // ELIGIBLE INSTRUMENTS

    Approved Contracts Only

    Trading is restricted to listed benchmark equity index futures (e.g., E-mini S&P 500, E-mini Nasdaq-100) and specified commodities during active market hours.

  4. RULE 04 // OBJECTIVE SCORING

    Risk-Adjusted Performance

    Leaderboard rankings balance total return alongside consistency metrics and maximum intraday exposure.
