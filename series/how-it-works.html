How the Series works

A step-by-step overview of tournament enrollment and simulated competition windows.

  1. STEP 01

    Registration

    Enroll during published enrollment periods. Launch events are free ($0 entry fee) and open to eligible market participants.

  2. STEP 02

    Sandbox Access

    When the event opens, authenticate into the Caldamere web environment to view market books, charts, and position metrics.

  3. STEP 03

    Execution & Telemetry

    Execute trades within established risk limits. Your real-time performance is audited and calculated on the event leaderboard.

Caldamere develops structured market competitions and practical projects for people who engage with financial markets.
