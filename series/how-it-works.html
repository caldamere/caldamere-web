Competition Protocol

How the Series Works

A simple three-step path from registration to competition.

View upcoming tournaments Review competition rules
  1. STEP 01 — REGISTER

    Enroll

    Enroll during the published registration window. Launch events are free to enter and open to eligible market participants.

  2. STEP 02 — ENTER THE SANDBOX

    Access

    When the event begins, sign in to the Caldamere trading environment to access live market data, charts, order books, and position metrics.

  3. STEP 03 — TRADE & COMPETE

    Execute

    Execute trades within the defined risk parameters. Performance is tracked in real time, verified, and reflected on the event leaderboard.

Caldamere develops structured market competitions and practical projects for people who engage with financial markets.
