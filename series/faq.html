Frequently asked questions

Key information regarding platform mechanics, execution, and series schedules.

Are tournament orders executed on real exchanges?

No. All orders execute within a high-fidelity simulated sandbox. Live capital is never deposited or traded.

How much does it cost to enter?

Launch tournaments are completely free ($0) to enter for all registered participants.

What instruments can I trade?

Instruments vary by event format, focusing primarily on highly liquid US Equity Index Futures (ES and NQ).

When will the Trading Journal launch?

The Caldamere Trading Journal is in active development. Features and access dates will be announced once core functionality is finalized.
