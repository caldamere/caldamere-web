Regulatory Disclosures

Standards, hypothetical execution boundaries, and platform operating parameters.

1. Simulated Trading & Zero Capital Custody

The Caldamere Series, its tournaments, software interfaces, and all associated analytical tooling operate exclusively within a simulated market environment. Under no circumstances do competition orders route to, execute upon, or interact with live financial exchanges, clearing houses, or designated contract markets (DCMs).

Caldamere does not accept, hold, solicit, or clear customer capital or margin funds for trading purposes. Participation in launch events carries no direct capital risk beyond the discretionary time expended by participants.

2. CFTC Rule 4.41 Statement

HYPOTHETICAL OR SIMULATED PERFORMANCE RESULTS HAVE CERTAIN LIMITATIONS. UNLIKE AN ACTUAL PERFORMANCE RECORD, SIMULATED RESULTS DO NOT REPRESENT ACTUAL TRADING. ALSO, SINCE THE TRADES HAVE NOT BEEN EXECUTED, THE RESULTS MAY HAVE UNDER-OR-OVER COMPENSATED FOR THE IMPACT, IF ANY, OF CERTAIN MARKET FACTORS, SUCH AS LACK OF LIQUIDITY. SIMULATED TRADING PROGRAMS IN GENERAL ARE ALSO SUBJECT TO THE FACT THAT THEY ARE DESIGNED WITH THE BENEFIT OF HINDSIGHT. NO REPRESENTATION IS BEING MADE THAT ANY ACCOUNT WILL OR IS LIKELY TO ACHIEVE PROFIT OR LOSSES SIMILAR TO THOSE SHOWN.

3. No Investment Advisory Services

Nothing contained on this website, published within tournament documentation, or generated by Caldamere software constitutes financial, investment, legal, tax, or accounting advice. Caldamere is not a registered Commodity Trading Advisor (CTA), Futures Commission Merchant (FCM), Introducing Broker (IB), or Investment Adviser with the CFTC, NFA, or SEC.

All historical charts, metric summaries, and leaderboard rankings represent comparative sandbox performance under uniform simulated conditions.

Caldamere develops structured market competitions and practical projects for people who engage with financial markets.
